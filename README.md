# Cinefora

## AI-Assisted Book-to-Screen Production Pipeline


Cinefora is an AI-assisted pre-production system designed to help turn books and prose into structured material for film and video production.


The goal is simple:


**Book → Production Bible → Scenes → Screenplay → Beats → Storyboard → Production Prompts**


Cinefora is not intended to automatically make an entire movie.


It is designed to give the writer/creator more control while using AI to handle the time-consuming work of extracting, organising and developing production information.

# What Cinefora Does


Cinefora can analyse written material and extract structured production information.


Depending on the source material, this can include:


### Characters


Character information such as:


* Name
* Age
* Appearance
* Physical characteristics
* Clothing
* Personality
* Behaviour
* Voice
* Character relationships
* Other information found in the source


### Locations


Cinefora can identify locations and extract information such as:


* Location name
* Appearance
* Environment
* Important details
* Function within the story


### Assets


Important objects and story assets can be extracted, including:


* Vehicles
* Technology
* Weapons
* Props
* Equipment
* Other significant objects


### Buildings and Structures


Cinefora can identify structures such as:


* Buildings
* Underground complexes
* Hangars
* Living quarters
* Facilities
* Other physical structures


### Spacecraft


Spaceships and other spacecraft can be extracted as structured production assets, including information such as:


* Shape
* Size
* Function
* Hull characteristics
* Propulsion
* Other source information


### Scenes and Story Material


The system can use the extracted information to help build:


* Scenes
* Screenplay material
* Story beats
* Storyboards
* Camera direction
* Shot information
* Lighting
* Movement
* Visual descriptions
* Production prompts

# Human Approval


One of the core principles of Cinefora is:


**AI proposes. The creator decides.**


Extracted information is presented for review before becoming part of the project's accepted production information.


This is important because AI can sometimes:


* misunderstand the source
* infer information
* fill in missing details
* interpret something creatively


Cinefora should therefore not silently decide what becomes canon.


The creator remains the final authority.


---


# Source Information vs AI Interpretation


Cinefora is being developed around an important distinction:


### Confirmed


Information explicitly found in the source material.


### Inferred


Information strongly suggested by the source but not explicitly stated.


### Unknown


Information that the source does not provide.


### AI Interpretation


A cinematic or creative interpretation generated by the AI.


### Approved


Information that the creator has reviewed and accepted into the project.


The long-term goal is for Cinefora to preserve this distinction throughout the production pipeline.

# Production Workflow


The intended workflow is:


```text
SOURCE MATERIAL
      ↓
AI EXTRACTION
      ↓
CHARACTERS / LOCATIONS / ASSETS / BUILDINGS / SPACECRAFT
      ↓
REVIEW & APPROVAL
      ↓
PRODUCTION BIBLE
      ↓
SCENES
      ↓
SCREENPLAY
      ↓
BEATS
      ↓
CONTINUITY
      ↓
STORYBOARD
      ↓
PRODUCTION PROMPTS
```


The production bible becomes the foundation for later stages.


The goal is to prevent every AI generation from starting from zero and inventing a different version of the same character, location or vehicle.

# Local AI


Cinefora is designed to work with different AI providers and can also be used with local models where supported.


Local AI is particularly interesting for manuscript analysis and extraction because it can allow writers to keep their source material on their own machine.


Cloud AI providers can be used where a stronger model is desirable for tasks such as creative development, screenplay generation or production prompting.


The exact capabilities depend on the model and provider being used.


---


# Privacy


Cinefora is being developed with a local-first approach.


Your manuscripts are your property.


Do not commit books, manuscripts, API keys or other private material to the GitHub repository.


If you use a cloud AI provider, the relevant text may be sent to that provider according to the provider's API and privacy policies.


Testers should therefore understand which AI provider they are using before processing private material.

# Current Status


Cinefora is **not yet a finished commercial product**.


It is an experimental production system moving toward a stable v1.0.


The current focus is reliability rather than adding large numbers of new features.


The priorities are:


* Accurate extraction
* Source grounding
* Human approval
* Consistent project data
* Character continuity
* World continuity
* Scene continuity
* Reliable exports
* Useful screenplay generation
* Useful storyboards
* Production-ready prompts


---


# What Cinefora Is Not


Cinefora is not currently intended to be:


* A complete video-generation platform
* A replacement for a filmmaker
* An automatic movie generator
* A cloud-based social platform
* A finished Hollywood production system


It is a **pre-production and story-to-screen development tool**.


The creator remains in control.


---


# Beta Goal


The goal of this private beta is to answer one simple question:


> **Can another writer take their own chapter, put it into Cinefora, and get genuinely useful production material back without having to fight the software?**


If the answer is yes, Cinefora will be one step closer to v1.0.


If the answer is no, the beta testers will help identify why.


That feedback is the purpose of this release.


---


## Built With AI


Cinefora itself has been developed with significant assistance from AI.


The project began from the idea of creating a controlled Book-to-Screen workflow for turning written stories into film-production material.


The aim is not to remove the human from the process.


It is to give the human **more control while allowing AI to do more of the repetitive work.**
